# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be respectful. Argue about code, not about people. Assume the person on the
other side is acting in good faith and is probably missing context rather
than being careless.

## What is expected

- Give feedback on the work: what is wrong, why it matters, and what would be
  better. "This retries inside the breaker, which hides failures from it" is
  a review. "This is sloppy" is not.
- Accept feedback the same way. A change request is not an accusation.
- Respect that people contribute in different timezones, in a second
  language, and around their day jobs. Silence usually means busy.
- Say when you do not know something. It is a maintained library, not an exam.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in issues, pull requests or
  anywhere else the project is discussed
- Discriminatory or demeaning comments about anyone's identity or background
- Publishing someone's private information
- Sustained disruption: reopening settled decisions to wear people down,
  derailing threads, or deliberate bad faith

## Scope

This applies to every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions,
commit messages, and any place someone is representing the project.

## Reporting

Report anything that concerns you to the maintainer at
**pinceladasdaweb@hotmail.com**. Reports are handled privately and only the
maintainer sees them. If the report concerns the maintainer, say so and it
will be handled with that in mind.

Depending on what happened, the response ranges from a private word to
editing or removing content, to blocking someone from the project. Whoever
reports something will be told what was decided.

Security vulnerabilities go somewhere else — see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
